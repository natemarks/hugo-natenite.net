We support live applications on an ongoing basis — not just deploy-and-walk-away. Every application we support comes with documentation, automated monitoring and testing, and on-call access to someone who actually knows the system.
<!--more-->

## Documentation

Every supported application has documentation covering how it's built, how it's deployed, and how to operate it — architecture, runbooks, and the reasoning behind key decisions. When something breaks at 2 AM, the answer shouldn't live only in one person's head.

## Monitoring automation

We instrument applications with automated monitoring and alerting so problems surface before a customer reports them — dashboards for the metrics that matter, alarms tuned to avoid both silence and noise, and synthetic checks that continuously exercise real user paths rather than waiting for something to actually break.

## Testing automation

Regressions get caught by an automated test suite running on every change, not by hoping someone notices in production. We build and maintain that automation as part of support, so confidence in a deploy doesn't erode as the application grows.

## On-call expert support

When something does go wrong, you reach someone who already knows the system — its architecture, its quirks, and its history — not a generic support queue starting from zero. On-call coverage means a real person responding to a real incident, not a ticket aging in a backlog.

## What you get

Documentation, monitoring, and test automation in place from day one, plus on-call access to the people who built and maintain it — so support means the application stays healthy, not just that someone answers the phone when it doesn't.
