We train your team on AWS using your own environment, not generic slideware. Sessions are hands-on, built around the accounts, pipelines, and applications your team actually runs, and every engagement leaves behind documentation your team can keep using.
<!--more-->

## Hands-on workshops

Short, focused workshops where your engineers build and break things in a safe sandbox: multi-account setup, infrastructure as code with AWS CDK, deployment pipelines with AWS CodePipeline, and the AWS security services. Each session ends with something working, not just a set of slides.

## Training on your own systems

We walk your team through the environments we build and support, covering how they're structured, why key decisions were made, and how to operate and extend them. New hires and existing staff can learn how your systems really work, not how a textbook example works.

## Operational readiness

Runbooks, incident walkthroughs, and on-call handoff practice, so your team can respond confidently when something goes wrong and isn't relying on one person's memory.

## What you get

A team that understands the AWS environment it owns, plus written material and recorded sessions to onboard the next person, so knowledge stays with your company rather than with us.
